## Planned four-keyframe structure

| Keyframe | Scale | Camera & focus | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kf1-micro` | `×20–×40` | Tight on one founding cell / hypha tip / seed; shallow ring-bokeh CoC | Single organism at unicellular scale; `legibility = 0.2` (wander) |
| `kf2-meso` | `×6–×4` | Pull back as colony branches; focus tracks the advancing front (`track: front`) | Colony spreads across the first stroke; filter-turret look transition |
| `kf3-macro` | `×2–×1` | Lock-on (`u = 0.35–0.40`); tilted focal plane across the word | Multi-organism communities snap into the strokes (`legibility → 1.0`) |
| `kf4-final` | `×1` | Near-flat focus (`CoC ≤ 3 px`) on the complete phrase, then ease-out | Full sentence holds legibly in house Rheinberg / darkfield HUDDI palette |
