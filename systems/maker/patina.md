## 2. How it works

- **State & arrival field:** 11 colony seeds placed on/around the word; geodesic arrival time `T` computed with `skimage.graph.MCP_Geometric` over a speed field derived from `legibility.food_field` (counters weighted at 0.012 so enclosed counters stay open).
- **Ownership & borders:** first-arrival Voronoi partition gives each colony its territory; dark prothallus contact lines form along boundaries where `|T_i - T_j|` is small.
- **Thallus & fruiting:** concentric rings modulated by arrival-time bands; outer hyphal margin grown with the shared `mycelium.grow` step; Voronoi areolate cracking inside older thallus zones; ~340 cup-shaped apothecia spawned behind the advancing front.
- **Optics:** weathered limestone relief with V-cut letter grooves and front-tracking tilted ring-blur CoC (`maxr = 11`).

## 3. Glyph coupling (`GlyphSource`)

Reads `mask`, `sdf`, `counters` (`legibility.food_field`), and `grad` (for V-cut bevel lighting). Any serif or epigraphic font, drawn path, or SVG works.

## 4. Known gaps & next refinement
