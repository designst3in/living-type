## 2. How it works

- **Space colonisation (primaries & secondaries):** auxin sources Poisson-sampled ~3.6 px apart inside each glyph (`≥ 1.5 px` from the outline), with line-of-sight visibility checks so veins never jump across counters; one petiole seed per connected glyph component; Murray's-law pipe-model radius accumulation (`r³ = Σ r_i³`).
- **Areole loop closure:** ~1,400 minor-vein tips run on `mycelium.grow` inside the glyph to close polygonal areole loops.
- **Lamina lifecycle (reverse/erosion arc):** chlorophyll lamina floods out from the veins clipped to the glyph mask, turns autumn bronze, and decays areoles-first (lowest vein-proximity first) until only the translucent vein skeleton remains; marginal vein strength is tied to `legibility`.

## 3. Glyph coupling (`GlyphSource`)

Reads `mask`, `sdf`, `counters` (line-of-sight counter barrier), and `per-letter ids` (one petiole per component).
