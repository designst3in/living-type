## 2. How it works

- **Dual-font slot layout:** the 8 letter slots of *contrast* carry both typefaces registered to shared glyph boxes; mycelium seeds on the left (*c-o-n-t*), Physarum on the right (*r-a-s-t*).
- **Contest dynamics:** `physarum_word.py` agents and `mycelium.grow` hyphae advance toward the centre, make contact at ~2.9 s, stall along the *r|a* boundary (3.5–5.2 s), surge into *r*, and push back to a contested equilibrium.
- **Optics:** darkfield HUDDI (`mycelium` in ink/cream, `Physarum` in warm ochre, `#33ff66` green on active hypha tips and plasmodial front).

## 3. Glyph coupling (`GlyphSource`)

Reads paired `GlyphSet` masks/SDFs registered per letter slot (`per-letter ids`) and `legibility.food_field`.

## 4. Known gaps & next refinement
