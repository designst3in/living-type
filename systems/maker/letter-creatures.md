# Letter creatures (`letter-creatures`)

> **System notes page** (generated by `sync.sh` from the clip inventory and script notes).
> Individual letters behave as soft-body ciliated micro-creatures (internal distance/bending springs, area-pressure conservation, boid steering, and metachronal cilia) that swim, jostle, and rearrange from the word *design* into *scientist*.

- **Source script:** `visuals/maker/letter-creatures/letter_creatures.py`

## 2. How it works

- **Soft-body mesh:** each glyph contour is resampled into a closed ring of mass points linked by edge springs, bending springs, and an internal pressure force proportional to signed contour area error.
- **Locomotion & morph:** boid separation/alignment plus target-slot attraction steers letters from the initial word layout to the target word while cilia beat along the perimeter.

## 3. Glyph coupling (`GlyphSource`)

Reads `outline paths` (resampled closed contours) and `per-letter ids` / target layout origins.
