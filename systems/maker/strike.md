## 2. How it works

- **Laplace potential:** glyphs are held at ground potential `φ = 1` and the top boundary at `φ = 0`; the harmonic breakdown field is combined with fBm turbulence and `legibility.food_field`: `food = 0.03 + 3.5·φ³·(1 + 0.8·fBm) + 1.6·food_field(l=0.80)`.
- **Stepped leaders & return stroke:** 4 leader tips launch from the top on the shared `mycelium.grow` branching engine; first contact at 3.4 s fires a white return-stroke flash + bloom, followed by Lichtenberg tree fill across each letter over 0.45 s, an afterglow floor `0.16 + 0.30·l`, and restrikes at 5.7 s and 7.3 s while losing sky branches decay in ~0.12 s.
- **Optics:** darkfield HUDDI with `#33ff66` green restricted to active leader tips and strike contact points.
