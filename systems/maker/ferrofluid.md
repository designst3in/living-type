## 2. How it works

- **Heightfield:** magnetic potential concentrated along the glyph skeleton/SDF; above the critical magnetisation threshold, a hexagonal/modulated carrier wave raises sharp Rosensweig cones along the strokes.
- **Render:** CPU heightfield raymarcher with grazing Fresnel environment reflections and circle-of-confusion blur.

## 3. Glyph coupling (`GlyphSource`)

Reads `sdf` and `skeleton / centreline` (single-line stroke paths). Works cleanly with PencilKit strokes or bold display faces.
