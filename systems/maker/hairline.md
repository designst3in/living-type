## 2. How it works

- **Skeleton tour:** each letter's medial-axis skeleton graph is decomposed into branches connected by smooth Bézier glides; one ciliate per letter traverses the tour at constant speed and swims off frame at the end.
- **Engraving wake:** glyph pixels unlock as the cell passes their nearest spine timestamp (`t_spine`), filling thick stems and hairline serifs with parallel hatch lines (~2.5 px pitch) that cool from cream wake to `#e8e8e8` ink.
- **Cell rendering:** slipper-shaped SDF body with 172 metachronal cilia along the pellicle, pulsing contractile vacuoles, and darkfield HUDDI `#33ff66` green restricted to the macronucleus; front-tracking tilted ring-blur CoC (`maxr = 7`).

## 3. Glyph coupling (`GlyphSource`)

Reads `skeleton / centreline graph`, `sdf`, `mask`, `per-letter ids`, and `feature points` (ball terminals and i-dot).

## 4. Known gaps & next refinement

Trail reveals by nearest centreline time rather than the cell body sweeping wide stems; cells start in sync; fine hatch lines can moiré in heavy compression. **Next:** sweep thick stems with the cell body, stagger per-letter start times, and anti-alias hatch spacing against local stroke width.

## 5. Port to macOS Swift/Metal
