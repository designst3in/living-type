## 2. How it works

- **Material recipe:** two-layer 2.5D gel heightfield (subsurface-scattering orange foam core + clear refractive outer gel shell) over a black background with Fresnel rims and internal micro-bubbles.
- **Strand mechanics:** filaments bridge separating letter contours; thickness follows catenary necking with Rayleigh–Plateau sinusoidal beading along the strand axis; v2 adds thicker letter gel, core lift, wetter foam, and one late filament snap.
