# Balloon (`inflate` G4 & `PICA` V6)

> **System notes page** (generated by `sync.sh` from the clip inventory and script notes).
> Letters inflate as pressurized soft-body balloons with elastic overshoot, shared flat film walls where neighbours press together, dome refraction, and an internal amber vein network; in V6 (*PICA*) the stacked balloons pop in sequence.

## 2. How it works

- **Inflation & contact:** each glyph SDF inflates into a pressurized dome `Z(x, y)` with damped harmonic overshoot and wobble; adjacent glyphs share a Voronoi contact plane that flattens into a soap-film partition wall.
- **V6 pop sequence (`pica_clip.py`):** stacked letters P/I/C/A inflate past their threshold and rupture one by one, opening a retracting tear lip and launching ~80 gel droplets + 45 specks.

## 3. Glyph coupling (`GlyphSource`)

Reads `mask`, `sdf`, `per-letter ids` (for per-letter pressure, pop schedule, and shared contact walls), and `outline paths`.

## 4. Known gaps & next refinement

G4 interior is slightly smoky and veins are static; V6 tear is wedge-shaped and remaining letters don't relax into freed space after a neighbour pops. **Next:** grow internal veins with the Physarum/mycelium sim, propagate a curved tear front, and release neighbour contact constraints after each pop.
